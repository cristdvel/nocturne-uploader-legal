youtube-ia · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

youtube-ia is a private tool used by a single individual (the "developer") to upload videos and edit the metadata of the developer's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not collect data about anyone else.

1. What the app accesses

When the developer authorizes the app with a Google account, it requests these scopes of the YouTube Data API v3:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: to upload videos to the developer's own channels.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube: to set thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags and scheduling on the developer's own videos.

The app reads the basic details of the authorized channel (its name and ID) to confirm that it is acting on the intended channel. It does not read comments, subscribers, watch history, or any other person's data.

2. What is stored

The only data stored is an OAuth refresh token per authorized channel, kept in a private file with restricted permissions on the developer's own computer. The video files and metadata that the developer chooses to upload are the developer's own content and stay on the developer's computer until the developer uploads them.

3. How data is used and shared

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the features described above, for the developer's own channels. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train machine-learning models. No human other than the developer reads it.

youtube-ia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Retention and revoking access

The refresh tokens are kept until the developer deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by removing "youtube-ia", or by deleting the token files on the developer's computer. After revocation the app can no longer act on the channel.

5. YouTube Terms of Service

By using the YouTube API Services through this app, the developer is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
